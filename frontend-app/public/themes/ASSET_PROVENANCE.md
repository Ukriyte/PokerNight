# Generated theme assets

These decorative assets were generated in ChatGPT and supplied by the project owner as a single 2×2 contact sheet on 2026-09-01. They were cropped along the sheet's white gutters and converted from PNG to quality-78 JPEG for runtime delivery.

| Runtime asset | Purpose | Source quadrant |
|---|---|---|
| `midnight-felt.jpg` | Midnight Casino table surface | top left |
| `broadcast-felt.jpg` | Poker Broadcast table surface | top right |
| `classic-card-back.jpg` | Unused card-back concept | bottom left |
| `four-color-card-back.jpg` | Unused card-back concept | bottom right |

The source prompts requested:

- A seamless, low-contrast dark emerald casino felt with no objects, text, logos, or borders.
- A seamless, low-contrast deep navy technical-fabric poker texture with no objects, text, logos, or borders.
- A symmetric midnight-blue and antique-gold card back with geometric linework and a central spade medallion.
- A symmetric deep-navy card back with cyan, red, blue, and emerald accents around an abstract four-suit medallion.

Card faces and runtime card backs remain deterministic inline SVG generated by application code. The AI card-back concepts are intentionally not referenced by the application because their dense generated details are unsuitable at playing-card size.
